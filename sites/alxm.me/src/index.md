## From founder to executive coach

Amidst the chaos and challenges of running a [Silicon Valley startup](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kenrickcai/2022/04/14/twingate-series-b-400-million-taking-down-vpn/), at what many in Western society would consider the pinnacle of a business career, I felt like I was living someone else’s life. I had driven myself to achieve everything I set my mind to, but the results felt hollow. I was incredibly proud of the product and team that I had built, but why did "success" still feel so unsatisfying?

During this period, looking for answers to my dissatisfaction, I started working with a therapist. Over the course of several years, I began to understand how to [listen to myself](/writing/2024/07/02/seeing-things-clearly/) at a deeper level, trusting the answers that were already there. I was shocked to discover how much of my behaviour and decisions had been unconsciously shaped by the views of others.

What my therapist practiced and modeled was an authentic capacity for unconditional acceptance of my experiences. Her acceptance of me exactly as I was allowed me to start to do the same for myself. My emerging self-compassion gave me the confidence to make choices in alignment with who I am, which not only changed [my own experience](/writing/2025/06/17/a-personal-biography/), but also how I related to everyone else.

This experience gives me the conviction that life-altering change and self-actualisation are available to anyone with the courage to fully meet themselves. Though it can be difficult work at times, the reward is nothing short of the ultimate journey of what it means to be alive.

It brings me great pleasure to witness and support this process in others through my [executive coaching](/coaching) practice. I bring an unusual combination of skills to my clients: the therapeutic lineage I inherit from my own therapist and my supervisor, best-in-class coaching training from CTI, and more than 20 years of business and product design experience.

## Cultural background & influences

I grew up in four countries (Mexico, the US, Spain, and England) before the age of 18, and this unusual upbringing opened up my acceptance to the wide variety and nuance of people’s experiences. I’ve seen how arbitrary cultural customs, rules, and thought patterns are, allowing me to more clearly see the individual personal experiences behind them. There are no objective rights and wrongs. We live in a world of our own subjectivity and making within which we seek to understand life’s meaning.

The majority of my childhood was spent in Spain and England, and I was born in Mexico to a Mexican mother and American father. “Home” is a difficult concept to identify for me, but England and Northwestern Europe come closest. I grew up speaking both English and Spanish, and I have varying degrees of proficiency in Swedish, German, French, and Mandarin Chinese.

Spiritually, I am not religious, but I've nurtured a meditation practice for many years, and I recognize the truth and timeless wisdom in Buddhism’s ancient teachings. They are as relevant today as they were several millennia ago. To deeply accept oneself and see the inherent beauty of the world without judgement is a continual practice.

## Building thoughtful products

Before reshaping my life’s priorities, I spent [nearly 20 years](https://www.linkedin.com/in/awmars) founding and working for some of the most recognizable brands and technology companies in the world, including [Twingate](https://www.twingate.com/), [Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com), and [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.net). I believe that a product is at its best when the customer is least aware of it, and my specialty is designing intuitive, thoughtfully-designed products that “just work” for users.

This experience is so rare in the software market that products that support, rather than frustrate, users’ needs are lauded as “magical” in their experience. Building these types of powerful experiences are the result of deep empathy with target customers, elevating design thinking to a high level of importance across engineering, product, and design, and having strong opinions on how you want your customers to experience you.

I enjoy bringing this experience into session with my [executive coaching](/coaching) clients, and I still get a rush from the thrill of finding elegant and creative solutions to product problems.

## Creativity and self-expression

In addition to my love and curiosity for working with people in all of their beautiful complexity, I am an [artist](/artwork), drawing or painting from life, usually with graphite, charcoal, or oil paint as the medium. I see art as a relational activity between self, subject, and viewer. My preferred subjects are nature and the human figure.

Our subjective experiences of the world are unique and irreducible, and what art holds the capacity for is the communication of experience from the artist to the viewer through the chosen subject and medium. The variability in art's interpretation is its power, not its shortcoming. Only through the most honest expression of our experience can we see the complexity and messiness that underpins our reality.
