As an executive coach, I support leaders’ personal growth as they navigate different stages of a successful, and often highly demanding, career. Over more than [two decades](https://www.linkedin.com/in/awmars) in the software industry, including as a VC-backed entrepreneur, I found that outside support was instrumental in helping me break patterns and understand myself more fully.

If you currently find yourself stuck at a plateau in leadership or executive performance, are facing a critical career or company juncture, struggle with the balance and influence that work and home have on each other, or feel you would benefit from speaking in confidence to someone who’s been there before, [let’s talk](/contact).

## What I offer

My approach involves working with all aspects of your life as they present themselves. The way we show up in the workplace is primarily influenced by our life experiences, and our work impacts the rest of our life in turn. Though we will look at what you feel is currently limiting you in the workplace, [my experience](/writing/2025/06/17/a-personal-biography/) is that truly meaningful growth can only come from following the roots of those limitations, wherever they may lead.

What my clients value most, beyond my warm, unconditional presence, is being able to draw from my diverse range of experience as required in the moment.

{% highlights %}
**20+ years in the software industry**. From startup to large corporation, public to private, entry-level to C-suite, sales to engineering roles, hiring and team-building, and navigating mergers, I’ve worn many hats. Benefit from my broad understanding across the industry.

**VC-backed startup founder**. Being a Silicon Valley startup founder is unlike any other experience in business. I have firsthand experience of the immense pressure, extreme acceleration of responsibility, fundraising challenges, existential crises, executive team formation, and co-founder relationship struggles.

**Product & design leader**. I’ve built product and design teams from the ground up and have launched products used by millions of people worldwide. I bring a rare combination of engineering, design, psychology, and commercial factors into product strategy.

**Trained coach and therapist**. I offer a full breadth of approaches to help my clients listen to the answers they already hold within themselves. I draw from both therapeutic and coaching approaches as the moment demands in our work together.
{% endhighlights %}

I trained as a coach with [Co-Active](https://coactive.com), the world’s leading coach training programme, and my approach is also heavily influenced by the [humanistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanistic_psychology#Counseling_and_therapy) school of psychotherapy.

The creative, present-moment approach that I bring to my work is inherited from my practice supervisor, who has over 30 years of experience as a therapist. His own teacher, [James Bugental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bugental), was the founder of existential-humanistic psychotherapy, and it is this legacy that I bring into the presence I offer you.

## What to expect

{% blockQuote "Carl Rogers", "On Becoming a Person", "https://www.abebooks.co.uk/Becoming-Person-Rogers-Carl-Constable/16269127392/bd" %}[…] we cannot change, we cannot move away from what we are, until we thoroughly _accept_ what we are. Then change seems to come about almost unnoticed.{% endblockQuote %}

Both my personal experience and what I see in my work with clients suggest that the answers and clarity we seek are already within us. Through the power of a supportive and unconditional relationship, everyone has the capacity to make contact with an inner wisdom that they can learn to trust as their guide.

Our accumulated experiences can obscure this clarity, creating beliefs and patterns that may have outlived their usefulness. My role as your coach is not to advise or direct, but to encourage and support you as you bring [awareness](/writing/2024/07/02/seeing-things-clearly/) to these unconscious scripts and turn them into conscious choices.

For example, conflict avoidance, over-preparation, or micromanaging tendencies may show up in the workplace as unhelpful behaviours, but have roots in other parts of your life. Exploring and integrating these areas is courageous work, and the results have the potential to be nothing short of life-changing.

Most of the work we’ll do together will take place in our sessions, though I may recommend that you do some exercises on your own. While there is no fixed time commitment, I generally work with people over a longer-term relationship, which honours the space and time that this type of work can require. Our sessions will take place weekly over Zoom.
