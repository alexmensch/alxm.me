As an executive coach, I support leaders’ personal growth as they navigate different stages of a successful, and often highly demanding, career. Over more than [two decades](https://www.linkedin.com/in/awmars) in the software industry, including as a VC-backed entrepreneur, I found that outside support was instrumental in helping me break patterns and understand myself more fully.

If you currently find yourself stuck at a plateau in leadership or executive performance, are facing a critical career or company juncture, struggle with the balance and influence that work and home have on each other, or feel you would benefit from speaking in confidence to someone who’s been there before, [let’s talk](/contact).

## What I offer

My approach involves working with all aspects of your life as they present themselves. The way we show up in the workplace is primarily influenced by our life experiences, and our work impacts the rest of our life in turn. Though we will look at what you feel is currently limiting you in the workplace, [my experience](/writing/2025/06/17/a-personal-biography/) is that truly meaningful growth can only come from following the roots of those limitations, wherever they may lead.

What my clients value most, beyond my warm, unconditional presence, is being able to draw from my diverse range of experience as required in the moment.

## What to expect

{% blockQuote "Carl Rogers", "On Becoming a Person", "https://www.abebooks.co.uk/Becoming-Person-Rogers-Carl-Constable/16269127392/bd" %}[…] we cannot change, we cannot move away from what we are, until we thoroughly accept what we are. Then change seems to come about almost unnoticed.{% endblockQuote %}

Both my personal experience and what I see in my work with clients suggest that the answers and clarity we seek are already within us. Our accumulated experiences can obscure this clarity, creating beliefs and patterns that may have outlived their usefulness. When we start to feel stuck in an area, what we're experiencing is our growth bumping into these established patterns, like a wheel falling into a rut.

My role in our relationship is not to advise or direct you, but to encourage and support you in bringing [awareness](/writing/2024/07/02/seeing-things-clearly/) to your unconscious patterns so that you may transform them into conscious choices. By exploring what arises in unconditional safety, you will begin to see your choices more clearly. With greater awareness comes the confidence to make new choices and see different results.

For example, conflict avoidance, over-preparation, or micromanaging tendencies may show up in the workplace as unhelpful behaviours, but have roots in other parts of your life experience. Exploring these areas, their history, and how they may have helped you in the past involves listening to yourself deeply and honestly. It is courageous work, and the results have the potential to be nothing short of life-changing.

Most of the work we’ll do together will take place in our sessions, though I may recommend that you do some exercises on your own. While there is no fixed time commitment, I generally work with people over a longer-term relationship, which honours the space and time that this type of work can require. Our sessions will take place weekly over Zoom.

Our work together is always confidential, and I adhere to the International Coaching Federation (ICF) [Code of Ethics](/assets/files/icf-code-of-ethics-2026-05.pdf).
